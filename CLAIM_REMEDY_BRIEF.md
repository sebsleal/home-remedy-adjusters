# Claim Remedy Adjusters Brief

Research date: 2026-03-23

## Company Summary

Claim Remedy Adjusters is a Florida public adjusting firm. They are not a repair or remediation company. Their role is to represent homeowners and other policyholders during property damage insurance claims by:

- Inspecting damage
- Documenting losses
- Preparing claim support materials
- Negotiating with the insurance carrier
- Guiding the client from inspection through settlement

Their positioning is homeowner-first, professional, clear, and advocacy-driven.

## Core Services

The site consistently presents these claim types:

- Water damage
- Hurricanes and storm damage
- Roof leaks and wind damage
- Mold and structural issues
- Fire and smoke losses

## Main Offer / Value Proposition

Recurring promises across the site:

- Free claim review
- No recovery, no fee
- Licensed public adjusters
- More than 10 years of hands-on claim experience
- Detailed inspections and documentation
- Direct negotiation with insurance carriers
- Ongoing communication throughout the process
- Representation for policyholders only, not insurance companies

## Audience

Inference from the site copy:

- Florida homeowners dealing with stressful property damage claims
- People who feel overwhelmed by the insurance process
- Homeowners concerned about underpaid, delayed, or denied claims

## Important Website Build Notes

- The staging URL appears to be a redesign or draft version of the live branded site at `claimremedyadjusters.com`.
- The staging homepage currently has `noindex, nofollow`, which is normal for staging but must not ship on production if SEO matters.

- The staging site is stronger than the live site in page depth because it includes individual service pages and a dedicated team page.
